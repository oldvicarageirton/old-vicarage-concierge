House Guide

A few important details

No smoking or vaping

Please keep the whole house smoke- and vape-free.

Pets

Please keep pets downstairs and off soft furnishings.

Hot tub

Use the plastic glasses provided and secure the cover whenever the tub is not in use.

Kitchen

Use the cork protectors for hot pans and the supplied oven sheets when cooking.

Waste and recycling

Separate cans, glass and plastics. Put full bags in the appropriate outside bin or storage unit.

Breakages

Please report any breakage, however small, so it can be replaced for the next guests.
