Looking After The Old Vicarage

Thank you for helping us care for our home.

We have put a great deal of care into creating a place where families and friends can relax, celebrate and make wonderful memories together. These helpful notes simply keep the house looking beautiful for every guest.

Phil & Claire

01

Fresh air indoors

Smoking and vaping are not permitted inside the house.

02

Four-legged friends

Pets are welcome downstairs. Please ensure no pets make it upstairs and are kept off all soft furnishings.

03

Leaving things as you found them

Please treat the house as your own. Kitchen cupboard photographs show where items belong; leave used items in the dishwasher or return them to the correct cupboard.

04

Toilets

Only toilet paper should be flushed. Please use the sanitary disposal bags provided and dispose of them in the bin.

05

Protecting furniture & worktops

Use the coasters provided for drinks and the cork protectors for hot pans. Hot pans placed directly on the worktop can damage it.

06

Ovens

Please use the Teflon sheets provided in all ovens when cooking, except when using the microwave function.

07

Electronics

Ask us before connecting games consoles, Fire TV sticks or similar equipment. Cupboards or parts marked with red tape must remain closed, and nothing may be plugged into the Cinema Room system.

08

Hot tub

Replace the cover and secure all clips whenever the hot tub is not in use. For safety, use only the plastic glasses provided around the hot tub.

09

BBQ

Please clean the BBQ after use in the plastic bowl provided, rather than using the kitchen sinks as the heavey metal parts will damage them.

10

Outdoor cushions

Protect outdoor soft furnishings from rain and birds. In the hot tub area keep the cushions undercover whenever they are not in use and in the rear garden store them in the shed provided.

11

Waste & recycling

Separate cans, glass and plastics from general waste and cardboard using the kitchen bins. Put full bags into the black bags provided, then into the correct outside bin or garden storage unit in the courtyard. Please never leave bags outside, or food waste int eh utility with the back door open — the local wildlife will have a feast.

12

Bedding & towels

Before departure, place used bedding and towels in the laundry bags stored in the bottom bedroom drawers. Mattress and pillow protectors can remain in place.

13

Courtyard parking

Please do not drive or park on the patio area unless using the EV charging station, just one vehicle at a time in this area.

14

Breakages

Let us know about any breakage, however small, before leaving so we can replace it in time for our next guests.

Finally

Enjoy every moment.

Thank you for helping us look after The Old Vicarage. We hope your stay is everything you wished for and that we have the pleasure of welcoming you back.

Phil & Claire

Phil & Claire recommend

Best family day out

Muncaster Castle

One of our absolute favourite days out. Muncaster is full of fascinating and magical things to discover, with something for every generation. The gardens are beautiful, the castle is magnificent and the birds of prey display is genuinely unforgettable. Allow a full day so you can enjoy it without rushing.

Phil & Claire’s tip

Check the display times when you arrive and plan your day around the birds of prey show — it is the highlight for us.

Get directions Official guide

Directions start from The Old Vicarage, CA19 1YH, and open in Google Maps.

Best scenic day out

Wastwater

Wastwater is magnificent. We love parking just before YHA Wasdale Hall, on the right-hand grass verge, where a discreet public footpath leads through the woodland to the boathouse and a quiet pebble beach. It is a beautiful place to sit by the water, swim in the invigorating lake or paddleboard across its narrowest part.

Phil & Claire’s tip

Visit early in the morning or later in the day, when it is quieter and the light over the fells is at its best. Afterwards, stop for lunch at the Sawmill and sit on the veranda beside the river.

Best heritage journey

The La’al Ratty

This little railway holds many happy family memories for us. We used to drive to Eskdale, board an open carriage at Dalegarth and ride down to Ravenglass — while one of our little ones, not so little now, tried to throw his dummies from the train. At Ravenglass we would stop for coffee and ice cream, explore the village and museum, and let the children play in the park.

Phil & Claire’s tip

Choose an open carriage when the weather is kind. Take your time in Ravenglass, then catch the train back to Eskdale and finish the day with an evening meal at The Boot Inn.

Our hidden gem

Emerald Pools

We only discovered the Emerald Pools recently, but they are something to marvel over. On the way, call into St Olaf’s Church, often described as England’s smallest parish church. The walk beneath Great Gable is surrounded by stunning scenery and is not too strenuous for reasonably active guests.

Phil & Claire’s tip

Take sturdy footwear and a towel. On a warm, sunny day, the crystal-clear emerald water is extremely difficult to resist — though it will still feel wonderfully fresh.

Best seaside afternoon

Seascale Beach

Late afternoon often takes us to Seascale, when the tide is perfect for rock pools, kite flying and a paddle in the sea. The beach seems endless, and you can walk all the way towards the sand dunes at Drigg. It is a fantastic place for dogs and almost guarantees that both the children and the dog will sleep well that evening.

Phil & Claire’s tip

Pop into Mawson’s for waffles, ice cream, cakes or sandwiches. For a proper seaside supper, stop at Salt for local fish and chips before heading back to the house.
